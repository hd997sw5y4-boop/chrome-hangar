Chrome Hangar — credits

Game design, code, rockets, hangar, sound and music
  Made for Ezequiel Cortez. Every rocket part, the hangar, test stand, pad and tower
  are built procedurally in code. All sounds and the ambient music are synthesized
  live with the Web Audio API. No recorded samples are used.
