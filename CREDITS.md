Chrome Hangar — credits

Game design, code, rockets and structures
  Made for Ezequiel Cortez. Every rocket part, the hangar shell, test stand, pad, tank farm, water
  tower, masts, bunker, viewing stand, trucks and fences are built procedurally in code. Engine
  roar, music and most sounds are synthesized live with the Web Audio API.

Terrain — real-world elevation and imagery, U.S. government public-domain works
  Elevation: USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP), The National Map — https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program
  Aerial imagery: USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP) and USGS ImageryOnly basemap, via
  USGS The National Map — https://www.usgs.gov/programs/national-geospatial-program/national-map
  Based on the Guadalupe–Nipomo Dunes coast, California, mirrored east–west and with the site graded flat.
  The range is fictional: not a real launch complex, and no real facility is depicted.
